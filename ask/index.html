“How humid is too humid for a desktop humidor”

Short answer: sustained humidity above 72 percent is too humid. That is the line where mold risk starts climbing and cigars begin to smoke wet: tight draws, uneven burns, and wrappers that split when they dry back out.

The working range most smokers aim for is 65 to 70 percent, with 68 to 70 the sweet spot for a desktop box. A brief spike to 71 or 72 on a hot day is weather, not a problem. What matters is where it lives day after day. If your desktop humidor sits at 73 or higher for days, air it out for 15 to 30 minutes, check that the humidifier is not overfilled, and use a drier pack for a while, a 65 percent pack will pull a wet box back down. And trust a calibrated digital hygrometer over the dial on the lid; those dials can read several points off.
