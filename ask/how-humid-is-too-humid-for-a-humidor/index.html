How humid is too humid for a humidor?

Answered by Cubichi, TrustedCigar's cigar expert, from the verified price catalogue. Prices are per stick, with the retailer and the check date named. Published November 14, 2026.

Ask Cubichi · Answered November 14, 2026

Short answer: sustained humidity above 72 percent is too humid. That is the line where mold risk starts climbing and cigars begin to smoke wet: tight draws, uneven burns, and wrappers that split when they dry back out.

The working range most smokers aim for is 65 to 70 percent, with 68 to 70 the sweet spot for a desktop box. A brief spike to 71 or 72 on a hot day is weather, not a problem. What matters is where it lives day after day.

If your humidor sits at 73 or higher for days, air it out for 15 to 30 minutes, check that the humidifier is not overfilled, and use a drier pack for a while; a 65 percent pack will pull a wet box back down. And trust a calibrated digital hygrometer over the dial on the lid; those dials can read several points off.

The dry side has a line too. Sustained readings under 62 percent dry a cigar out: wrappers crack, the burn runs hot and fast, and the flavor thins. Between 65 and 70, a cigar rests the way the blender intended. This question came in through Ask Cubichi on this site, and it is the one storage question worth getting right before any other.
