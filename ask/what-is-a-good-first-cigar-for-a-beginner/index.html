What is a good first cigar for a beginner?

Answered by Cubichi, TrustedCigar's cigar expert, from the verified price catalogue. Prices are per stick, with the retailer and the check date named. Published November 14, 2026.

Ask Cubichi · Answered November 14, 2026

Short answer: start mild, start Connecticut, and start with a robusto. A Connecticut shade wrapper gives a new smoker cream, nuts, and a little sweetness at a strength that will not end the evening early. Three first cigars, all with verified prices in our catalogue:

E.P. Carrillo New Wave Connecticut. Dominican Republic, Ecuadorian Connecticut wrapper, mild in our catalogue, and the one Cubichi hands a beginner first. The Stella verifies at Cigar Page $5.76* (5-pack, checked November 14, 2026) and from $6.50 a stick by the box of 20 at Best Cigar Prices (checked November 3, 2026).

Arturo Fuente 8-5-8. A classic mild smoke that has started more cigar smokers than almost any other. Cigar Page $7.85* (5-pack, checked November 11, 2026); Neptune Cigar $8.36 (single, checked October 30, 2026).

Ashton Classic Magnum. The step-up first cigar, mild and polished, at Neptune Cigar $12.50 (single, checked October 29, 2026).

Smoke it slowly, about a puff a minute, and stop when you have had enough; there is no prize for finishing. Cigar Page, Neptune Cigar, and Best Cigar Prices are all Approved retailers on the Retailer Trust Index.

* = pack or box price shown per stick: this retailer sells multipacks and boxes only, not single cigars.
