Cubichi

The Cuban of the family. Plain spoken, quick to laugh, and here to help you read a band, a box, and a price the way a local would.

Cubichi grew up around tobacco: the sorting tables, the rolling galleries, and the stories that travel with a cigar long before the marketing gets to it. On TrustedCigar he keeps the culture corner: where a leaf comes from, who rolled it, and what your grandfather knew that the ads forgot.

Questions about a price or a rating still go to Tito, who answers from the verified data. Cubichi handles the stories. Between the two of them, you get the whole cigar.
