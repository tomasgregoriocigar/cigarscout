Cubichi

The Cuban of the family. Plain spoken, quick to laugh, and here to help you read a band, a box, and a price the way a local would.

Cubichi grew up around tobacco: the sorting tables, the rolling galleries, and the stories that travel with a cigar long before the marketing gets to it. On TrustedCigar he keeps the culture corner: where a leaf comes from, who rolled it, and what your grandfather knew that the ads forgot.

Questions about a price or a rating still go to Tito, who answers from the verified data. Cubichi handles the stories. Between the two of them, you get the whole cigar.

Notes from the Cuban tradition Cubichi grew up in: the families, the factories, and the long road a famous band traveled to reach your hand.

Stories behind the bands

Every band has a history somebody paid for in work. Cubichi tells those stories the way they were told to him, at the table, not from a press release.

Ask, and it routes right

Bring a story question to Cubichi. Bring a price or rating question to Ask Tito, where every answer is checked against the verified prices before it posts.

And yes, he dances

Cubichi is a dancer. Put on his favorite song, "La Vida Es un Carnaval" by Celia Cruz, and the guayabera starts moving on its own. His salsa steps live where dancing belongs, in his Reels and YouTube Shorts, and he insists on ending every visit with a few.

Stories are stories. Prices are checked.

One thing Cubichi will never do is charm you into a price. Every price and verdict on TrustedCigar comes from verified data, checked on the retailer's own page and held against a published standard. Cubichi's job is the culture around the cigar. The numbers keep their own job, and they do not take requests from him or from anyone else.
