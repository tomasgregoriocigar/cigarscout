Week 3

Most Smoked, Best Priced

The cigars people talked about this week, with every verified price we hold.

How a cigar makes this chart: people are smoking it and talking about it, in the groups, on the forums, in the press, and we hold at least one verified price for it. Prices are per stick, read from the retailer's own page. Where we hold more than one price, every one of them shows. Where a price is not from this week, the row says so.

An honest note on this week: it was a short week for cigar talk. The conversation in the groups was our 1,000-smoker survey, not cigars, and only three cigars cleared both bars. Rather than pad the chart with cigars nobody discussed, this edition runs short, with the three that earned their place.

Prices verified Friday, November 20, 2026, except where a row shows an earlier date. Prices change, so check the retailer's page before you buy.
