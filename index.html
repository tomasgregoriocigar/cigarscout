Our goals, stated plainly

TrustedCigar has two goals, and we'd rather state them than hint at them.

For consumers: a better buying experience — honest per-stick prices, retailers rated on published evidence, and billing surprises exposed before they reach your card.

For retailers: a fair chance to improve what consumers experience. Every rated retailer receives its full scorecard — its weakest pillars, and exactly what qualifying takes — and when a retailer climbs, the climb is published. The standard isn't a wall to keep stores out. It's a ladder: shoppers get stores they can trust, and stores get a clear, honest path to earning that trust.
